About Charm Capsule

Charm Capsule is a sovereign, multi‑chain governance and identity system. Designed for clarity, permanence, and control, it unifies:

  • 📡 Multi‑chain snapshots
  • 🗳 Governance proposals & rules
  • 🔗 Chain metadata & validators
  • 📦 Capsule identity & sovereignty model
  • 📱 PWA offline‑first architecture

Sovereign Philosophy

Charm Capsule is built on a simple principle: your identity and governance must remain yours.

Every module — snapshots, governance, metadata, routing, offline mode — is designed to be:

  • 🛡 Self‑contained
  • 🌙 Minimal
  • 🔐 Sovereign
  • 📜 Transparent
  • 🌐 Multi‑chain aware

Technical Architecture

The capsule runs on a modular PWA architecture:

  • ⚙ SPA Router (`router.js`)
  • 📄 Snapshot Viewer (`snapshot-viewer.js`)
  • 📚 Snapshot List Loader (`snapshot-list.js`)
  • 🏛 Governance Engine (`governance.js`)
  • 🧬 Capsule Identity Viewer (`capsule-info.js`)
  • 📦 Offline Engine (`sw.js`)
  • 📱 PWA Manifest (`manifest.json`)

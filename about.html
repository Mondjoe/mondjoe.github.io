About Charm Capsule

Charm Capsule is a proof‑driven governance layer designed for multi‑chain ecosystems. It unifies validator data, governance snapshots, and capsule‑based decision flows into a single, elegant interface.

Mission

To create a transparent, verifiable, and modular governance experience that works across chains — empowering validators, contributors, and communities with clarity and control.

Design Philosophy

Minimal. Modular. Proof‑first. Every component is built to be portable, auditable, and future‑ready.

Roadmap

• Validator viewer • Snapshot explorer • Governance capsule engine • Multi‑chain integration • Real‑time alerts
